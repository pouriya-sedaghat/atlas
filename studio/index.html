Travel profile

Arrows show the one-way direction for the selected profile, along each road’s own coordinate order. The overlay shows what the source said about that profile’s access. The two are separate facts: a prohibited road still shows its arrow. Switching profile re-reads the data already loaded; it does not query the server again.

Access records what the source said, not whether a route may use the road. That judgement belongs to a routing profile, which Atlas does not have yet.

Feature inspector

Road topology

Topology says which road paths are structurally connected and where they split. It is not a routing graph, and a segment is not evidence that any mode may traverse it: direction, access and speed are facts on the road feature, joined by roadFeatureId. Two points are connected when they share a source identity — never because their coordinates match or their lines cross.
